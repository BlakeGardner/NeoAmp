# NeoAmp's built-in MilkDrop presets

These 98 presets are built into NeoAmp (see
[src/milkdrop/built_in.rs](../src/milkdrop/built_in.rs)). They were picked
from the "Cream of the Crop" collection of 9,795 MilkDrop presets, curated and
sorted by ISOSCELES and published by the projectM project at
<https://github.com/projectM-visualizer/presets-cream-of-the-crop> (commit
`0180df21f5e0`). The files are as they are there, but for spaces at the
ends of a few names.

A preset's name begins with who made it, before the " - ": several names
joined by "+" or "&" made it together, or one remixed another's. The
collection's folder each came from is its category.

The collection's licence note says that MilkDrop presets were, in almost all
cases, released under no particular licence, so that each author holds the
copyright in their presets; that since they were released freely and have
been used in many programs for two decades, they can be taken to be in the
public domain; and that any author who does not want their preset there can
ask for it to be removed. The same goes here: an author who would rather a
preset were not built into NeoAmp can open an issue, and it will be taken out.

NeoAmp chose these by drawing every second preset of the collection that
needs no pictures of its own, with a test signal for sound, and keeping
lively, colourful ones across the categories and authors.
